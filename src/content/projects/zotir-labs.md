## I led with the outcome, not a service list

Most agency sites open by listing what they do. I did the opposite. The headline leads with what a client actually wants, a website that brings enquiries, and one they can edit themselves. The services sit to the side, quieter. Someone lands and knows in five seconds whether this is for them.

## One brand, built as a single thing

The logo, the colours, the type, the page structure and the code were one project, not a hand off between five people. That is why it holds together from the first screen to the last. Navy, cream, and a single coral accent. Bricolage Grotesque for the big type, Manrope for reading. No stock photos, no filler.

## The decision I care about most, the owner runs their own site

Small business sites die because the owner cannot change them. A new price, a new photo, a new post, and they are stuck waiting on a developer. So I built a simple editor over the content. The team changes a line and hits publish. No ticket, no wait. That is the difference between a site that stays current and one that quietly goes stale.

## Everything points at one action

The whole site has one job, book a free review. No contact maze, no ten field form. Booking is a short guided flow instead of a cold form, so it feels like the start of a conversation rather than a wall.

## Fast on a real phone, light or dark

I tested on a mid range phone on 4G, not the laptop it was built on. It carries a local time chip for a worldwide audience, copy a twelve year old can read, and a full dark mode.
