About

Ahmad Farooq

Systems engineer. I build websites and the systems behind them.

I'm Ahmad, 20, based in Lahore. I build websites and the machinery that makes them work: CRMs, dashboards, automations, AI agents. Most people pick a side, frontend or backend, design or systems. I don't. The website and the engine behind it are one job to me.

I'm self-taught. I learned by building things that had to actually run, not by collecting certificates. Kai, a ten-agent assistant that handles outreach, habits, and a live inbox, built in three days on Python and SQLite. A hand-tracking 3D viewer that runs in the browser. A scanner that reads a landing page and tells you what's costing you customers. A donation platform for a nonprofit. The through-line is range: I'll design the brand, write the code, wire the automation, and ship the whole thing.

I run two companies. Zotir Labs, where we build brands and websites for owner-led businesses, and Kaivex Systems, for local SEO and e-commerce growth. When I'm not building, I write about making things, and about the quiet habit of outsourcing our thinking to machines.

You won't find me on camera. You'll find the work, and it holds up.
